Who's behind this

I'm Alekh Chandra Sahoo, from Puri, Odisha, India — currently pursuing my MTech in Computer Science and Engineering at Odisha University of Technology and Research. I completed my BTech in Computer Science and Engineering at the Government College of Engineering, Kalahandi (GCEK), Bhawanipatna. INGENIOUX doesn't exist as a company — it's the name I work under, and this site is the lab notebook for what I'm building toward under it.

I like building software that actually solves a problem instead of just demonstrating one. Most of my time goes into understanding and building AI/ML models, and into research around cloud computing and data structures & algorithms — how systems store, move and reason over data at scale, and how the underlying algorithms make that efficient or not. The projects below are the practical side of that: lost-and-found retrieval systems, classroom platforms, assistants — each one a place to test an idea against something real rather than leave it on paper.
